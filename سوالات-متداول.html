Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about safety and uptime.

Is my data safe on P K?

Yes. Accounts are protected with end-to-end encryption and blockchain-based verification.

What happens if the service goes down?

You can check live status anytime from the Status tab at the top of the page.

Is it safe to download from Cafe Bazaar or Myket?

Yes, both are distributed directly through the official Cafe Bazaar and Myket apps.

Does the PWA update automatically?

Yes. The web app loads the latest version every time you open it, no manual update needed.

How do I report a security issue?

Use the contact details listed on the Privacy Policy page to reach the P K team.
